Who we are

PollPapa is a social media polling platform that promotes freedom of speech and expression, and lets people vote on important social, political, environmental, cultural, and other issues.

Vote Today Influence the World Tomorrow

Welcome to our platform! PollPapa provides you with easy-to-use tools to create and participate in polls and surveys on the topics that matter most to you. Tell your truth, connect with like-minded people in your circles and channels, and discover new perspectives that inspire you.

PollPapa is an innovative platform where polls connect with respondents quickly and easily. With over 10 different poll types, PollPapa provides you with valuable feedback from the precise audience you need to reach. The real-time results we provide are recorded onto the blockchain, ensuring that every voice is heard and making an impact. Subscribe and join our growing community!

Where real people are really heard.

PollPapa provides a platform where all voices are heard, and real people get honest answers. Quit raising your company's success with guesswork, and start asking real questions to real people. Let’s work together to make the unknown known.

Find out what your customers really want.

Your consumers' opinions and beliefs about your company are the most critical information you can have. With PollPapa, you have access to practical resource that lets you ask tough questions and receive honest responses.

Be "For the People" by polling "The People".

Serving a community means understanding what its people need. PollPapa closes the gap between the people and their leaders by providing a platform that is easy to use and delivers honest opinions, not biased beliefs.

Blockchain-driven results.

With today's media propaganda, finding honest opinions and information is nearly impossible. Poll results are often skewed, and information tends to be gathered through manipulation and biased platforms. Our polls are user-generated, recorded on the blockchain, so it is fully transparent, uncensored, and decentralized.
